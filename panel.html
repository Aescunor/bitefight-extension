Battle Simulator v1.6.10 ? 📖 About Simulator

This tool simulates battles in Bitefight based on game rules — attack order, unit types, tier special abilities and damage calculation. It helps build and test formations before real combat, as well as auto-optimize army composition.

✓ ~99% accurate from layer 60 onward — validated against live battle reports.

⚠ Lower layers may diverge slightly. Simulator is not liable for higher unit or Blood Essence losses than predicted. We recommend verifying with a small test before deploying large armies. 🔔 🔔 Recent Updates
